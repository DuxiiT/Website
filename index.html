FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about using Aether and managing your access.

How do I execute Aether in-game?

Copy the loader command above, paste it into your compatible executor, and run it. Aether will automatically detect the game you are in and load the corresponding script.

How do I obtain a free key?

When running a free script, click “Get Key” inside the interface. Follow the instructions to receive your key, then paste it in to unlock the session.

Do I need Premium to use Aether?

No. The Free tier offers full access to our free game scripts. Premium gives you keyless execution, access to exclusive scripts, and priority support.

How frequently are game scripts updated?

Scripts are updated as games receive patches. Most updates roll out within hours to maintain uninterrupted compatibility.

Can I request a new game?

Yes. Join our Discord server and submit your suggestion in the community requests channel.
